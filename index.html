Impact Factor, Article Influence Score and each journal’s position in its category, 2023–2025. Hover over a row, or tap it, to see how the journal changed between years.

About the data

Impact Factor (IF) and Article Influence Score (AIS)
Journal-level values: a journal has the same IF and AIS in every category it is listed in.
Rank in category
The journal’s position in a category, ordered by AIS (1 is highest). “39 / 107” means 39th of 107. Category sizes change from year to year, so positions are also shown as “top x%” (rank divided by category size), which can be compared across years.
Quartile
The faculty quartile based on the rank, shown as supplied in the source files. D1 is a top-decile label that appears in the 2024 and 2025 files only. To compare years, change over time counts D1 within Q1.
Change since the previous year
Compares a record with the year immediately before it: its quartile band moved up, moved down or stayed the same. A record missing from the previous year is reported as not listed, never as zero, and years are not skipped.
Missing values
A dash means the journal was not listed in that category that year. A zero is a value the source reports as zero.
Journals across years
Journals are matched by name, ignoring capitals, “&” versus “and” and hyphens. The files have no ISSN, so a journal that changed its title appears as separate entries.
